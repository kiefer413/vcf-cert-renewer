# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone. Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption,
and publishing another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently exclude participants whose behavior is harmful.
Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer contact
mechanism. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This concise policy is based on the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
